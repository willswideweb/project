This is Blorpo, my pet rock. I adopted him in July 2003, thinking it would be a great idea.

It was not.

Immediately he became a nuisance more than a companion, always hungry, pooping everywhere,
throwing temper tantrums, and, above all, devastating my financial account.

Even though he can’t move, it became very clear I couldn’t take care of him. I’ve decided to abandon him.

I rented an apartment in Pennsylvania, opened the wall, put him and a live camera inside, and sealed it shut.
The live camera feed has been embedded.

The only reason he’s being broadcasted live is so I can see if something bad happens to him.
If something happens to him, email me at will@willswideweb.github.io immediately.

His exact whereabouts will remain undisclosed to keep him safe.
