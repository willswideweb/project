for background, me and my partner visited a hotel in the west side of north america. we booked for the fourth floor, room 414. once we entered the room we immediatley began mapping out the room and setting up our equipment. live cameras were placed in the thermostat, bathroom, alarm clock, closet, peephole and the hotel pool. along with this we placed an audio recorder behind the television (click anywhere on screen to hear live audio) we then preformed camera tests in the room and embedded the feeds to this page. we then checked out and went back to our hometowns.

as of today the room is unoccupied by guests but we expect this to change in the following months. it is reccomended you regularly check back on the webcams to see if anyone has checked into the room. sadly our pool cam has recently gone offline. though it should be a quick fix, unfortunatley the amount of people that visit the pool regularly makes it unlikely we will be able to fix it anytime soon without getting caught. these webcams can run for years on end, provided they are not tampered with. to avoid tampering, the exact hotel will not be stated. we apologize for the low quality of the feeds however any higher quality camera would get caught almost immediatley, these feeds are closer to images that are updated periodically (1 per 5 sec)

Thermostat Cam (Top Left feed)

this webcam was rigged into the pre-existing hotel room thermostat. it is arguably the most detailed webcam, capturing the bed, nightstand, and the curtain and windows. it offers life view of where someone would be sleeping during the night. when the thermostat is used, the camera automatically goes offline to prevent detection, and uses motion sensors to determine when to go back online. this was the first camera we installed.



Bathroom Cam (Top Middle feed)

this nearly microscopic camera was attached in the door handle of the bathroom door from the inside. it offers a live view of the toilet and part of the shower. this camera is highly regulated given its nature and what it would catch on camera, however, most of the time it is online and uncensored. unexpectedly, this camera is the most commonly watched in our database.



Alarm Clock Cam (Top Right feed)

this camera was placed inside the hotel's alarm clock. it offers a live view of the channel or status of the telvision. it is also the only camera to film the bed next to the bathroom wall. it would capture the feet of those sleeping in either bed. if someone were to move the alarm the camera would unfortunatley be at a different angle. the camera temporarily goes offline once the alarm goes off to avoid detection.



Closet Cam (Bottom Left feed)

this camera is at the southeast corner of the closet. it offers live view from inside the closet. most of the time this camera is the least interesting of the six, however every once and a while we expect cool or interesting things to happen. if the room is occupied the camera may possibly be obscured by clothes owned by the guest.



Peephole Cam (Bottom Middle feed)

this camera has been placed in the peephole of the hotel room entrance door. it peers out into the hotel hallway and the neighboring room's door. this feed is one of the two cameras that films the outside of room 414, offering live view of the hallway. most of the time the hallway is empty but every once in a while it would catch a janitor or passerby, or even guests entering the neighboring room.



Pool Cam (Bottom Right feed)

by some stroke of luck we managed to get to the hotel's pool for ~2 hours without anyone arriving, allowing us to set up our hidden camera in a corner and carry out tests. when we left the pool we saw a family literally seconds away from arriving, so we were lucky. this feed captrues live view of the hotel pool and is undeniably the most active of the cameras, frequently capturing multiple people of all ages playing in the pool. it also captures what goes down when the pool is closed for undisclosed reasons, which is cool.

Room 414 layout

we didnt just set up cameras while in the room, we took multiple photos and videos of the room to construct a model of the room for better visualization. from what weve seen most rooms on the fourth floor are very similar to this layout and it is assumed the room seen in the peephole feed has an identical layout.




Live Audio

our live audio recorder is hiddden behind the television and is constantly recording audio 24/7. unlike our cameras it records very high quality recordings. it is disguised as a piece of television hardware but is the most vulnerable of our devices. as of today all it captures is blank noise, however it would capture talking and other conversations when the room is occupied. unfortunatley the nature of the recorder results in a pause in the audio every few seconds, we are activley trying to fix this. if you have clicked on the page you should be hearing the audio (unless my idiot partner embedded it wrong.)
